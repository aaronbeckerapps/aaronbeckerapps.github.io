Rise Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

Aaron Becker Apps (“we,” “our,” or “us”) built the Rise app as a commercial application. This page is used to inform visitors and users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decides to use our Service.

By choosing to use our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not directly collect, store, or sell your personally identifiable information (such as your name, address, or phone number) on our own servers.

However, the app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device, process transactions, and ensure app stability.

Third-Party Services

Our app relies on the following third-party providers, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core app functionality, security, and updates. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Billing: Used to process in-app purchases and upgrades securely. We do not receive or store your credit card or payment details; all transactions are handled directly by Google Play.
  • Google APIs (e.g., Time Zone API): Used to provide accurate local time or regional features within the app.

2. Device Permissions

  • Internet Access: Required to verify in-app purchases, load content, and communicate with Google Play Services.
  • Location Data: Used only while the app is active, and only when you choose to use your current location. Your location is sent to Google APIs (such as the Time Zone API) to provide accurate local time or regional features. We do not store your location, and we do not share it with any third parties other than Google as described above.

3. Data Security

We value your trust in using our app. Because we do not maintain external user accounts or private databases, your app preferences and purchase statuses are stored locally on your device or managed securely through your Google Play account. Please remember that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
